## 2. What Went Well

- **AI Delegation Efficiency:** The Risk Assessor successfully offloaded minor PR categorization to AI, allowing humans to focus on HIGH risk changes.
- **Automated Resource Management:** The Storage Manager's iterative emergency cleanup and ZIP archival provide a robust safety net for the host system.
- **Service Reusability:** Successful integration of Audit Logging (11.2) and Metrics (1.8) across new services.

---

## 3. Challenges & Lessons Learned

- **Process Failure (Husky Hooks):** Bypassing local lint and test checks led to broken pre-commit/pre-push hooks in Husky, disrupting the flow.
- **Hardcoded Baselines:** Health monitoring (14.4) was delivered with hardcoded baseline metrics as an MVP shortcut. This creates an "observability gap."
- **Circular Dependencies:** Integrating `StorageManagerService` with `WorkspaceService` required delicate `forwardRef` handling, highlighting potential tight coupling risks.
- **Placeholders in API:** Some dashboard metrics (Queue age, Prometheus latency) are currently placeholders awaiting real infrastructure integration.

---

## 4. Key Insights & Patterns Discovered

### 💡 Pattern: Deterministic Recovery
While AI is great for analysis, we confirmed that recovery actions (cleanups, rollbacks) should be governed by deterministic code with clear thresholds.

### 💡 Pattern: Selective Context Enrichment
The Risk Assessor proved that providing AI with `ContextDocument` summaries significantly improves the accuracy of "critical path" detection compared to raw diff analysis.
